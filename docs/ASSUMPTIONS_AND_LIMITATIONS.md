# Assumptions and Limitations

## Assumptions
- The project uses a small synthetic district inventory network focused on demonstration-quality analytics.
- Seasonal variability and procurement delays are approximated, not measured from the field.
- Lead-time behavior is modeled rather than derived from real stock orders.

## Limitations
- Synthetic data should not be used as operational evidence.
- Missing data is flagged and treated cautiously; it does not imply a confirmed stock-out.
- The current prediction models are baseline examples, not production-grade forecasting systems.
